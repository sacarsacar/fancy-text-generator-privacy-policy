Privacy Policy: Fancy Text

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Fancy Text is an Android app for styling text with fancy fonts, symbols and frames, published by Himalayan Code Works. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version: what you type, save and copy never leaves your phone. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics. The app sends one thing: an anonymous crash report if it crashes, so we can find and fix the bug.

What stays on your phone

Everything you do in Fancy Text stays in the app's private storage on your phone: the text you type, your saved favourites, the list of recently copied text, and your settings (theme, vibration, sound and where you put the menu button). We cannot see any of it, and it is not backed up to us. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Your clipboard

When you tap a style, the app copies that text to your phone's clipboard so you can paste it elsewhere. The app reads your clipboard only when you tap Paste, and only to put that text in the input box.

Photos

The Image card tool can use a photo as its background. When you tap Use a photo, Android's own photo picker opens and you choose one picture. The app uses it only to draw the card on your phone. It does not upload the photo or keep a copy after you leave the screen, and it cannot see your other photos.

When you tap Share image, the card is handed to Android's share menu. Where it goes next is up to you and the app you share it with.

Text from other apps

Fancy Text appears in Android's share menu and text-selection menu. If you send text to it from another app, that text opens in the app's input box on your phone and goes nowhere else.

Crash reports

The app uses Firebase Crashlytics, a Google service, to tell us when it crashes. A crash report contains:

  • the technical details of the crash: which part of the code failed, and the stack trace
  • basic device information: device model, Android version, app version, free memory and disk space, orientation, and whether the app was in the foreground
  • a random installation identifier created by Crashlytics, which lets us count how many devices a crash affects. It is not linked to your name, email, advertising ID or any account

Crash reports do not contain your text, favourites, photos, settings or anything you typed.

Reports are sent over an encrypted connection to Google, which processes them on our behalf under the Firebase data processing terms and the Google Privacy Policy. Crashlytics keeps crash data for 90 days, then deletes it. We use it only to fix bugs. We do not sell it, share it with anyone else, or use it for advertising.

What we do not do

  • No sign-in, account or profile
  • No advertising and no advertising ID
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or files
  • No in-app purchases

Permissions

The app requests INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, used only to send crash reports, and VIBRATE, used for the gentle taps you feel when you copy or save (you can turn these off in Settings). It asks for no runtime permissions.

Links

Settings → About links to our website, the developer's website, our email address and this policy. Tapping a link opens your browser or mail app, and that site's own policy applies.

Children

Fancy Text is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Crash reports contain no personal information.

Your choices and deletion

Crash reports are not linked to you, so we cannot pick your reports out from anyone else's. Crashlytics deletes every report automatically after 90 days. To stop any report being sent, uninstall the app. Your favourites, recent copies and settings are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android settings.
